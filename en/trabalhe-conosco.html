Our origin

A history built with courage.

In the early 1980s, in a challenging market, our founder, Jorge Hanaoka, decided to combine his entrepreneurial experience with the purpose of offering high-quality products. That's how Hanaoka was born: from scratch, driven by courage, vision, and a commitment to excellence.

Our first product was Sulfuric Acid PA, and from the very beginning, the pursuit of quality and high purity was at the core of the company.

Over 40 years of history, we've grown, professionalized, and kept evolving. We've invested in modernization, innovation, continuous improvement, and in developing the people who build this journey with us. Today, Hanaoka Soluções Químicas combines four decades of experience with the drive to keep shaping the future.

Chemistry that transforms

Social responsibility · Educando para a Vida.

At Hanaoka, we believe our impact also happens outside the company. That's why we support the Educando para a Vida project, which serves children and teenagers in socially vulnerable situations.

We run initiatives such as the Winter Campaign, donating clothes and blankets, and the Christmas Drive, delivering boxes of chocolates to children.

Small actions, big transformations. Because caring for people is also part of who we are.
