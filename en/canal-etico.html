Integrity

Ethics Reporting Channel.

Ethics, respect, and transparency are part of who we are. At Hanaoka Soluções Químicas, we believe trust is built through integrity, respect, and accountability.

Our Ethics Channel is a space for reporting situations that may represent inappropriate behavior, violations of internal policies, or conduct contrary to Hanaoka's principles and values.

When to use it

When should you use the Ethics Channel?

The channel can be used to report, for example, situations related to:

  • Moral or sexual harassment;
  • Discrimination or disrespect;
  • Fraud, embezzlement, or conflicts of interest;
  • Corruption or unethical practices;
  • Violations of safety standards;
  • Non-compliance with internal policies and procedures;
  • Other conduct incompatible with Hanaoka's values.
Our commitment

Your report will be treated seriously.

Every report received is reviewed responsibly and with due care, seeking to preserve the confidentiality of the information and the people involved.

Hanaoka does not tolerate any form of retaliation against people who make good-faith reports.

We also expect the channel to be used responsibly, providing, whenever possible, information that helps with a proper investigation of the facts.

How to report

How do I file a report?

When submitting your report, try to include as much detail as you have available, such as what happened, when and where it occurred, the people involved, and any possible evidence or witnesses. You don't need to have all the information to file a report — share what you know.
