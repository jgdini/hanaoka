Ethics Channel.

Hanaoka maintains and encourages its own reporting channel so that employees, suppliers, customers, and third parties can anonymously report any violation of our policy or of applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

Reports submitted through this channel are received and handled as non-conformities by our Integrity Committee. If the reporting party identifies themselves, they may be contacted for help with the investigation and/or updates on the case.
