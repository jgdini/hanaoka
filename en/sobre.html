Jorge Hanaoka · Founder
Who we are

From a dream to a national reference in high purity.

Micro-Química was born from a dream. More than 40 years later, that legacy carries the founder's name: Hanaoka Soluções Químicas. The change is more than a rebrand — it recognizes a journey of learning, resilience, and continuous growth.

Today, we develop high-quality, high-purity chemical products through sustainable, technology-driven management, with our own analysis and research laboratory.

Hanaoka's policy

A commitment to quality and excellence.

An ethical, sustainable approach

We follow ethical, innovative, sustainable leadership in the chemical sector, offering safe, cost-effective solutions through continuous improvement of our Integrated Management System.

Environmental responsibility

We develop products and processes that optimize the use of natural resources and prevent pollution, managed on ethical practices and respect for human rights.

A safe work environment

We promote employee consultation and active participation in building a safe, healthy work environment for everyone.

An agent of social change

We contribute to education and collective awareness, seeking to generate a positive impact on the chemical sector and on society as a whole.
