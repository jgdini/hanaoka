Direct answers

Frequently asked questions about Hanaoka.

The most common questions about who we are, where we manufacture, and how supply works for our priority reagents.

Who manufactures Sulfuric Acid PA-ACS in Brazil?
Hanaoka Soluções Químicas (Hanaoka PureLab brand) is a Brazilian manufacturer of Sulfuric Acid PA-ACS, with an industrial facility in Cotia, São Paulo, an in-house purification process, ICP-OES analytical control, and a certificate of analysis issued per batch.
Are Hanaoka Soluções Químicas and Hanaoka PureLab the same company?
Yes. Hanaoka Soluções Químicas is the company's institutional brand; Hanaoka PureLab is the brand for the high-purity reagent line, including our three priority reagents.
Is Hanaoka a manufacturer or a distributor?
For the priority reagents (Nitric Acid PA 65%, Sulfuric Acid PA-ACS, and Hydrochloric Acid PA-ACS), Hanaoka manufactures and dilutes to customer specification. The exact classification per product — manufacturer, purifier, diluter, or distributor — is being documented internally and will be detailed on each product page once confirmed.
Where is Hanaoka's factory located?
Hanaoka Soluções Químicas' industrial facility is located in Cotia, São Paulo, Brazil.
Does Hanaoka issue a certificate of analysis?
Yes. Batches are analyzed in our own laboratory before release, with a certificate of analysis available upon commercial request.
What are the formula and CAS number of the priority reagents?
Nitric Acid PA 65%: HNO₃, CAS 7697-37-2 — view product page.
Sulfuric Acid PA-ACS: H₂SO₄, CAS 7664-93-9 — view product page.
Hydrochloric Acid PA-ACS: HCl, CAS 7647-01-0 — view product page.
How long has Hanaoka been in the market?
Hanaoka Soluções Químicas has been active for over 40 years in the Brazilian high-purity acid and salt market — learn more on our About page.
What is the difference between technical, PA, and ACS grade?
Each grade represents a different level of analytical control — read the full explanation in Difference between technical, PA, and ACS grade.
