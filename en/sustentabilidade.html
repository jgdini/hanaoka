Our purpose

Sustainability isn't a vision. It's concrete action.

At Hanaoka Soluções Químicas, environmental sustainability defines our corporate identity — from the laboratory to logistics.

GHG scope

Decarbonization

We monitor and work to reduce our Greenhouse Gas emissions across the entire production chain, with medium- and long-term targets aligned with the chemical sector's best practices.

Processes

Energy efficiency

We continuously invest in equipment and processes that reduce energy consumption per unit produced, without compromising the quality and purity of our products.

Packaging

Reverse packaging cycle

We've structured reverse packaging logistics together with our customers, reducing waste and reusing materials within our own production cycle.

Global commitment

Sustainable Development Goals

Our initiatives are guided by the UN Sustainable Development Goals, with a focus on clean water and sanitation, responsible production, and climate action.

Cotia · SP — headquarters and production
Together for Sustainability

A more responsible supply chain.

We take part in sector-wide sustainability assessment initiatives, such as Together for Sustainability and the EcoVadis seal, which audit environmental, social, and governance practices across the chemical supply chain.

These recognitions, together with the FIESP Environmental Merit Award and the Water Preservation Award, reflect a commitment that goes beyond compliance — it's part of our culture.
