High-purity reagent division

Hanaoka PureLab.

Hanaoka's high-purity, high-performance chemical reagent division — built for laboratories, industries, and critical applications that demand reliability, traceability, and technical excellence.

The result of over 40 years of Hanaoka know-how in the manufacture, handling, and control of chemical products, PureLab was created to concentrate all of that knowledge into a line dedicated to high-grade reagents — in-house manufacturing, start to finish.

In-house manufacturing

Processes carried out under strict criteria at our own industrial facility in Cotia, São Paulo.

ICP-OES control

Analytical verification of trace metal contaminants before every batch is released.

Batch certificate of analysis

Documented traceability, from the lab to delivery.

40+ years of know-how

Decades of Hanaoka's manufacturing and quality-control experience, concentrated in this line.

Hanaoka Soluções Químicas is the company's institutional brand; Hanaoka PureLab is the division dedicated to high-purity reagents. The exact regulatory classification per product is under internal review.

How we guarantee purity

From manufacturing to certificate, under our own control.

Every PureLab reagent goes through its own purification process and ICP-OES analytical control in our laboratory, before being released with a batch certificate of analysis — without relying on third parties to attest to the product's purity.
