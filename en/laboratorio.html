Quality control

Our own laboratory, at every step.

We don't outsource what guarantees our purity. Every batch that leaves Hanaoka passes through our own analysis laboratory before it reaches you.

What we do in here

From the bench to the released batch.

Batch-level quality control

No batch leaves the factory without internal analysis — the purity guarantee isn't a label promise, it's a bench result.

PA/ACS grade testing

We verify that every product meets the required analytical specifications — from technical grade to PA-ACS, per American Chemical Society standards.

New product development

New formulations and concentrations are born here before reaching production, always starting from a customer's real need.

Continuous improvement

Products already in circulation continue to be monitored and refined — quality isn't a one-time seal, it's an ongoing process.

Four decades of our own analysis and research laboratory, guaranteeing quality control for every batch — from developing new products to continuously improving those already in circulation.
