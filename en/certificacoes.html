Recognition

Certified. Audited. Proven.

We don't ask you to take our word for it. Every seal below represents an independent external audit of our processes, products, and practices.

ISO 9001

International quality management system certification — attests that our processes are standardized, documented, and regularly audited.

ISO 14001

International environmental management certification — proves that we manage our operation's environmental impact in a structured, ongoing way.

EcoVadis

An independent sustainability assessment that audits environmental, labor, ethics, and responsible-sourcing practices across the entire supply chain.

FIESP Environmental Merit Award

Recognition from the São Paulo State Industry Federation for outstanding environmental initiatives in the industrial sector.

Water Preservation Award

Recognition for the conscious use and preservation of water resources in our production processes.

DuPont Award

Recognition for safety practices in the handling and transport of chemical products, including the use of protective equipment like the Tychem® suit.

Together for Sustainability

A chemical-industry initiative for the assessment and continuous improvement of sustainability practices across the supply chain.

Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)

Our initiatives are guided by the UN agenda, with a focus on clean water and sanitation, responsible production, and climate action.
